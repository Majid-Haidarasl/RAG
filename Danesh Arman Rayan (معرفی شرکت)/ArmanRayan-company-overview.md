## 4. Divisions

### Administration & Corporate Resources
Handles people, money, contracts, and office operations: hiring, leave, compensation, talent retention, cost centers and project accounting, contract drafting and approval, office services, and official correspondence.

### Hardware & Equipment Services
- **Repair & Maintenance:** intake, diagnosis, three repair levels, 90-day repair warranty.
- **Procurement Advisory:** needs assessment, supplier comparison, written purchase advice.
- **Sales:** leads, pro-forma invoices, discount limits, delivery and invoicing.
- **Assembly, Test & Delivery:** parts checklist, ESD safety, stress testing, labeling, delivery report.

### Software & Digital Products
Teams are organized by **product delivery**, not by programming language. Frameworks are capabilities inside teams:

| Team | Core capabilities |
|---|---|
| Web Development | Django (default backend), Laravel (PHP clients), Node.js (event-driven/real-time), React (only web frontend) |
| Mobile Development | Flutter (React Native only for existing client code) |
| Windows & Desktop | C# / .NET |
| UI/UX Design | User research, wireframes, design system, design acceptance |
| Visual Identity & Graphics | Branding, logo design, file handover |
| Support & Deployment | SLA, incident priority, escalation, knowledge base |

## 5. Key Policies

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| Hiring | Needs request → VP and budget approval → ad (≥10 days) → HR screening → HR and technical interviews → 3-month probation |
| Leave | 26 working days annual; unpaid up to 30 days (VP) or 90 days (CEO); hourly up to 4h/day, 16h/month; remote up to 8 days/month |
| Raises | Up to 15%: VP + CEO; 15–25%: CEO; above 25%: Board only |
| Project bonus | Up to 1.5× monthly salary for profitable projects |
| Contracts | Over 500M toman: CEO approval; over 2,000M toman: Board approval |
| Sales discount | Sales rep up to 3%; VP up to 8%; higher needs CEO |
| Support SLA | P1: respond 30 min, workaround 4h; P2: 2h / 16 business hours; P3: 8 business hours / 5 business days |

## 6. Flagship Case: Azarpakhsh Warehouse System

- **Client:** Azarpakhsh Food Distribution (fictional)

- **Status (end of Shahrivar 1403):** 62% progress, 1,240M toman still to bill
